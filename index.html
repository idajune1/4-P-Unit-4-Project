Poems about lightbulbs, neurotransmitters, and the revolution

fisticuff on halloween

they'll be right next to you when your life is at its lowest, we can watch the stars get hotter, our bodies will burn together. I am a solider, I will fight for a cause I don't completely understand.

I will use imaginary words to explain right from wrong. the notation is index, the frequencies middle, and my fist palm. In conflict you will find a reason to care. my faith is being tested but so is everything else.

if infinity is true, then a infinite amount of things will happen. eventually, the universe will die, refigure itself , and rise again.

eventually, time will start repeating itself.

i will be born again, in a infinite amount of years from right now.

ill do things right the next time around.



captain savior

when did humans decide to bring the sun inside? everything we worship kindles. the almighty harnesses itself into lamps and fixtures. there is something electric, buzzing inside of every human being. does that go away when we die?

we are biological machines. the most cruel and unusual punishment of them all is to be born. haven't you noticed it? the next great apocalyse is coming, i hope i experience it. it will be illiumated, in a thousand hertz of fluorscence.

lightbulb , the shoes you have to fill!



the grime

we are all flesh jars of saliva. rooms inwhich our organs fill. humans have beaten their anatomy in so many ways. we live lifespans far longer than our ancestors. we have outsmarted the elements, animals, and tides. we have not outsmarted the desire to be loved.

i pray for a experimental surgery, one that will slice the string connecting my brain to my heart. i will no longer carry the burden of connection.

on friday night i entered the sixth circle of hell. ida june, self destruction, and the facts in writing!

my final verdict is not a flowery one.

we are all doomed to be consumed.
